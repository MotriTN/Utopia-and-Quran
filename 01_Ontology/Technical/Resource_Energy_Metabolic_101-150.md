# Resource, Energy, & Metabolic Flux: Variables 101-150

## Energy Harvesting & Thermodynamic Efficiency

- **UID:** SEQ-101
- **Variable:** [[Energy-to-Human-Calorie Ratio]]
- **Systemic Logic:** The efficiency of converting external energy (solar, fusion, etc.) into the utility required to sustain a human unit's existence and agency.
- **Theoretical Metric:** External Joules per Human Calorie equivalent.
- **Interactions:** [[Mitochondrial Respiratory Capacity]] -> THIS -> [[Existential Security Baseline]]
- **Emergent Cluster:** #Energy #Metabolic

- **UID:** SEQ-102
- **Variable:** [[Embedded Energy Transparency]]
- **Systemic Logic:** The visibility of the total energy cost required to produce, transport, and maintain any artifact or service.
- **Theoretical Metric:** Joule-denominated cost-label accuracy.
- **Interactions:** [[Information Provenance Transparency]] -> THIS -> [[Resource Waste Rate]]
- **Emergent Cluster:** #Energy #Signal

- **UID:** SEQ-103
- **Variable:** [[Thermodynamic Waste Baseline]]
- **Systemic Logic:** The minimum unavoidable entropy (heat/waste) generated by the civilization's metabolic processes.
- **Theoretical Metric:** Watts of waste heat per unit of GDP-equivalent utility.
- **Interactions:** [[Visual Complexity Balance]] -> THIS -> [[Metabolic Waste Heat Recovery]]
- **Emergent Cluster:** #Energy #Physics

- **UID:** SEQ-104
- **Variable:** [[Exergy Utilization Efficiency]]
- **Systemic Logic:** How much of the "useful" work potential of an energy source is actually captured before it degrades to ambient heat.
- **Theoretical Metric:** Second-law efficiency percentage.
- **Interactions:** [[Technological Innovation Rate]] -> THIS -> [[Natural Capital Depreciation Rate]]
- **Emergent Cluster:** #Energy

- **UID:** SEQ-105
- **Variable:** [[Energy Harvest Decentralization]]
- **Systemic Logic:** The degree to which energy collection is distributed across the nodes vs. concentrated in fragile hubs.
- **Theoretical Metric:** Gini Coefficient of Energy Generation.
- **Interactions:** [[Autonomy Quotient]] -> THIS -> [[Grid Resilience Factor]]
- **Emergent Cluster:** #Energy #Systemic

- **UID:** SEQ-106
- **Variable:** [[Grid Resilience Factor]]
- **Systemic Logic:** The ability of the energy distribution network to maintain function during localized failures or surges.
- **Theoretical Metric:** Systemic failure probability per node-outage.
- **Interactions:** [[Energy Harvest Decentralization]] -> THIS -> [[Mean Time to Energy Restoration]]
- **Emergent Cluster:** #Energy #Resilience

- **UID:** SEQ-107
- **Variable:** [[Peak Load Elasticity]]
- **Systemic Logic:** The system's ability to shift its metabolic demand in response to energy availability (e.g., solar cycles).
- **Theoretical Metric:** % of demand that can be deferred without utility loss.
- **Interactions:** [[Collective Attention Management]] -> THIS -> [[Systemic Stability]]
- **Emergent Cluster:** #Energy #Metabolic

- **UID:** SEQ-108
- **Variable:** [[Metabolic Waste Heat Recovery]]
- **Systemic Logic:** Capturing low-grade heat from industrial or computational processes for secondary use (e.g., heating, agricultural forcing).
- **Theoretical Metric:** % of waste heat diverted to secondary utility.
- **Interactions:** [[Thermodynamic Waste Baseline]] -> THIS -> [[Energy-to-Human-Calorie Ratio]]
- **Emergent Cluster:** #Energy #Metabolic

- **UID:** SEQ-109
- **Variable:** [[Energy Storage Density (Systemic)]]
- **Systemic Logic:** The capacity to "buffer" energy across time, decoupling harvesting from consumption.
- **Theoretical Metric:** Total Wh storage capacity / Mean hourly demand.
- **Interactions:** [[Hydrological Storage Buffer]] -> THIS -> [[Existential Security Baseline]]
- **Emergent Cluster:** #Energy #Resilience

- **UID:** SEQ-110
- **Variable:** [[Primary Energy Source Diversity]]
- **Systemic Logic:** Preventing over-reliance on a single harvesting modality (e.g., just solar).
- **Theoretical Metric:** Simpson’s Diversity Index of energy sources.
- **Interactions:** [[Cognitive Diversity Ratio]] -> THIS -> [[Systemic Resilience]]
- **Emergent Cluster:** #Energy
